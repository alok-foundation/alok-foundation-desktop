About Us

Who We Are?

Alok Foundation, a non-profit organization, uplifts underprivileged children through education, healthcare, and development programs.

Our Belief

“Don’t worry, the world will change on its own — you change one life, one thought.” — Sushmita Sen

Core Values

Transparency, Compassion, Integrity, Community Upliftment.

Our Team

Social workers, educationists, volunteers, and trustees.

Vision

“Spreading Light of Education”

When ordinary kids are empowered with education, they can do extraordinary things.

Mission

“Empowering Communities Through Education”

Help kids build strong educational foundations for a brighter future.
